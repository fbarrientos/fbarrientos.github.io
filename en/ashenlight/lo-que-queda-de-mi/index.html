What Remains of Me

Original Spanish edition: Lo que queda de mí (De acero a cenizas, de cenizas a luz)

A promise made in a coma, the loss of fingers, a doctoral dissertation written with a single functioning finger, and learning to walk again—twice. Fredy Barrientos recounts an experience of survival and rebuilding, as his body changes forever and life demands new ways to carry on.

In What Remains of Me, the narrative moves from the vulnerability of intensive care and grief over permanent physical loss to the patient recovery of thought, intellectual work, and day-to-day autonomy. With rigorous and contemplative honesty, the author examines the silent transformations of personal identity when pain and physical constraints become the starting point for a renewed life.

Far beyond an account of adversity, these pages offer a meditation on the value of time, memory, and the determination to find purpose among the ashes.

About Fredy Barrientos

Fredy Barrientos

Artificial Intelligence Researcher · Author

Fredy Barrientos is an artificial intelligence researcher and the author of What Remains of Me. He holds a Ph.D. from the Complutense University of Madrid and works on computer vision and generative models. His writing explores survival, changes in identity, and rebuilding a life.
