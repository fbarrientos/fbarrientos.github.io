What Remains of Me

Original Spanish edition: Lo que queda de mí

A promise made in a coma, the loss of fingers, a thesis written with a single functioning finger, and learning to walk again—twice. Fredy Barrientos recounts an experience of survival and rebuilding, as his body changes forever and life demands new ways to carry on.

About the Author

Fredy Barrientos

Artificial Intelligence Researcher · Author

Fredy Barrientos is an artificial intelligence researcher and the author of What Remains of Me. He holds a Ph.D. from the Complutense University of Madrid and works on computer vision and generative models. His writing explores survival, changes in identity, and rebuilding a life.
